### 💼 About Me

I don't just build systems — I design infrastructure that removes constraints. I focus on **Platform Engineering** and **Low-Level Systems**, building backbones that are predictable, efficient, and fully under control. I invest deeply in **observability, system design, and internal platforms**, so teams can move fast without losing reliability.

### 💡 Engineering Philosophy

- 🧱 **Break Constraints** — Eliminate unnecessary complexity and rigid systems.
- ⚔️ **Own the System** — Understand systems deeply, from low-level to distributed scale.
- 🌍 **Freedom at Scale** — Build platforms that empower developers, not restrict them.
- 🔍 **See Everything** — Observability is non-negotiable.
